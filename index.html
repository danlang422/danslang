a little about me

I spend a lot of time thinking about humans and humanity, and I believe our ability to imagine, communicate, and create together is our greatest strength.

I hope for a day when we can look past our differences and recognize our potential as a species — if we truly work together.

I try to approach any endeavor, personal or professional, with curiosity and enthusiasm. I'm passionate about sharing my learning and experiences because I believe knowledge and understanding empower us all.

I've found that open, honest communication — even around difficult topics — is vital as we work together to solve complex problems.

a better-than-Star-Trek future

Star Trek imagines a humanity that has overcome many of its struggles by the time the Federation comes into being. I'm hoping we can get closer to its utopian elements — without having to make the same mistakes as our fictional forerunners.

Someday — within my lifetime, hopefully — we'll be a planet where all creatures can live long, and prosper, together. 🖖
